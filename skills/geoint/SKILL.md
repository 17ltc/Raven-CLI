# GEOINT

Use `geoint_geocode` for a named public place and `geoint_reverse` for
coordinates supplied by the user. Report the provider, timestamp, precision,
and uncertainty. Do not infer a private person's location, track individuals,
or expose sensitive location data. Public place analysis must remain bounded
and descriptive rather than operational.
