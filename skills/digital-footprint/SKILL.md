# Digital Footprint Review

Confirm the subject and authorization. Minimize personal data, avoid doxxing,
do not correlate sensitive identities unnecessarily, and report only public
observations with source links and confidence. Prefer organization, domain,
brand, and project indicators over private-person profiling.
