# Advanced Passive OSINT

Use only public, user-authorized targets. Start with `web_search`, then use
`web_robots`, `web_page_profile`, `web_sitemap`, and bounded link extraction.
Respect robots.txt, terms of service, rate limits, and the strict result caps.
Never bypass authentication, paywalls, CAPTCHAs, or access controls. Do not
perform port scans, credential testing, exploitation, or mass crawling.

Preserve source URL, collection time, exact observation, and confidence. Keep
facts separate from inference and corroborate important claims with multiple
independent sources.
