# OPSEC

Use `opsec_scan_text` or `opsec_scan_workspace` to identify likely secrets
without returning their values. Use `redact_sensitive_text` before sharing
logs or messages and `url_privacy_review` before publishing links.

Minimize personal data, preserve evidence only when necessary, and distinguish
exposure risk from proof of compromise. Never silently upload, delete, or
modify user material. Travel, identity, privacy, and breach-response advice
must remain defensive and lawful.
